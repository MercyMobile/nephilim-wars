The Fallen Watchers

"Two hundred angels... descended upon Mount Hermon. Bound together by a solemn oath under the leadership of Semyaza... they took mortal wives and taught forbidden knowledge that would forever corrupt the earth".

Azazel

The Master of Bronze & Blades

Azazel revealed the secrets of warfare—how to forge swords, craft shields, and create armor. He also taught cosmetics and vanity, turning humanity's focus inward toward physical appearance.

Tradition: Warfare & Metallurgy

Revealed the sword, the breastplate, and the art of smithing.

Semyaza

Leader of the Two Hundred

Semyaza shared enchantments and root-cutting (herbalism and poisons), giving mortals power over life and death through plants. He bound the Watchers by oath on Mount Hermon.

Tradition: Enchantment & Root-Cutting

Taught the bindings of enchantments and the property of herbs.

Baraqel

The Star Reader

Baraqel taught astrology and the observation of stars, opening pathways to divination and fate manipulation. He revealed the constellations and their hidden meanings.

Tradition: Astrology & Divination

Revealed the course of the stars and signs of the sun.

The Soul Economy

Righteousness (RP)

Righteousness Points (RP) are earned through acts of virtue and faithfulness. They can be spent to reroll checks or heal allies using divine grace.

Corruption (CP)

Corruption Points (CP) represent spiritual degradation through forbidden knowledge and cruelty. High CP reduces Initiative and risks divine judgment.
