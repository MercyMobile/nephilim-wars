Mount Scopus: stone-vessel manufacturing workshop

Workshop with hundreds of chalkstone fragments, unfinished vessels and production debris · reported February 2026

Overview

Archaeologists identified a chalkstone vessel-production workshop in a cave east of Jerusalem after an investigation of antiquities theft.

Unfinished stone cups and bowls, waste fragments and lathe-turning marks document a production technique in Jewish society during the late Second Temple era.

What the evidence shows

  • Hundreds of fragments and production debris, together with unfinished forms, support workshop identification.
  • Turning marks on vessels document use of rotary shaping methods.

Open questions

  • The actual buyers and distribution network are not individually documented.
  • The workshop is contextual evidence for stone vessels, not a discovery of the six jars described in John.
