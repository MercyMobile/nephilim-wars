Gezer Water System

Rock-cut stepped tunnel to a spring cave · Middle Bronze Age

Overview

A keyhole-shaped shaft cut into the bedrock opens into a broad, barrel-vaulted stepped tunnel that descends steeply to a natural cave holding groundwater. Macalister cleared it over more than a month and published it as the 'Water-passage' (Excavation of Gezer I, 1912, pp. 256-265).

Macalister found only a few stray objects in the silt, noted flint-tool marks on the upper walls, and found no inscription. From the Late Bronze ('Third Semitic') material that closed the entrance and the worn state of the steps, he dated the cutting to about 2000 BCE at the latest and attributed it to the Canaanite city.

The tunnel was re-exposed and dug deeper in 2010-2018. The excavators date it to Middle Bronze IIB (c. 1750-1650 BCE), state that there was no exit from the system, and report finds they read as cultic and calendrical; their final report is The Canaanite Water System at Gezer (2025).

What the evidence shows

  • Keyhole-shaped entrance pit, 34 ft 6 in long, 7 ft 5 in wide at north end and 14 ft 6 in at south; steps cut in its floor (Macalister I, p. 258).
  • Tunnel entrance 'an imposing archway, 23' high and 12' 10" broad'; steps across the full width; barrel-vaulted roof (Macalister I, p. 259).
  • Water stood 94 ft 6 in vertically below the rock surface, about 130 ft below the ground surface at the time of excavation; 200 gallons drawn off did not lower it (Macalister I, p. 261).
  • The tunnel ends in a natural cave c. 80 x 28 ft; Macalister found no second opening (pp. 261-262).
  • 'No trace of an inscription of any sort was observed' (Macalister I, p. 262).
  • 2015 season: pottery showed 'a clear transition from Late Bronze Age pottery to Middle Bronze Age pottery dating between 1800 and 1500 B.C.' (Warner, quoted by BAS).

Open questions

  • Date: Macalister (1912) placed it c. 2000 BCE at the latest because Late Bronze silt sealed it and the steps were heavily worn; Dever (as reported by BAS 2015) had suggested Late Bronze or Iron Age; Warner, Yannai and Tsuk (2010-2018 excavations; final report 2025) date it to MB IIB from the ceramic sequence in the fill. The fill dates the tunnel's disuse; the construction date is inferred from what lies beneath it.
  • Purpose: Macalister thought it was begun as a siege exit and struck the spring by accident, because a vertical well would have been shorter by about 3 to 5; Père Vincent (in Macalister's note) held it was cut for water from the start. The 2025 report adds a cultic/calendrical reading of finds (reported by Pathway, Oct 2025); the finds themselves have not been examined for this entry.

The Find

  • DateMiddle Bronze Age IIB, c. 1750-1650 BCE for its construction per the 2010-2018 excavators; Macalister (1912) had already dated it to c. 2000 BCE 'at the latest'. Not Solomonic.
  • How datedPottery in the fill: the 2015 season found Late Bronze pottery above Middle Bronze pottery of 1800-1500 BCE (Warner, reported by BAS 2015); the final publication (Tsuk et al., 2025) dates the tunnel to MB IIB. Macalister reasoned from 'Third Semitic' (Late Bronze) silt closing the entrance plus wear on the steps.
  • Discovered1907-1909 (cleared); re-excavated 2010-2018, R. A. S. Macalister (PEF); re-excavated by Dan Warner, Eli Yannai and Tsvika Tsuk with Dennis Cole and Jim Parker (NOBTS with the Israel Nature and Parks Authority and IAA), Trench 29, inside the city near the 'First Semitic Palace' court, Tel Gezer
  • Now heldIn situ, Tel Gezer National Park, Israel
  • MaterialCut into bedrock limestone
  • DimensionsMacalister: entrance pit 34 ft 6 in long; entrance arch 23 ft high x 12 ft 10 in wide; tunnel slopes at 38-39 degrees from vertical; water level 94 ft 6 in vertically below rock surface; terminal natural cave c. 80 x 28 ft. BAS (2015) summary: shaft c. 138 ft long at 38 degrees.

Biblical Passages

  • 1 Kgs 9:15-17Names Gezer among Solomon's works. On every date proposed by the excavators (c. 2000 BCE; MB IIB), the water system predates Solomon by many centuries and is not part of that building work.
  • Josh 10:12-13The 2025 report's authors connect finds they read as solar/lunar cultic to the Aijalon Valley episode (quoted in Pathway, 2025). This is their reading; no text at the site names it.
