Is the Ark a capacitor?

Ex 25:11 overlays it with gold inside and out with acacia between — two conductors and a dielectric. But the Baraita says Bezalel made it as three nested boxes and capped the upper rims with gold, which joins the two shells and shorts it.

capped (as described)84.6 pF
isolated (if not capped)2487 pF
ratio29.4×

The construction the Baraita describes is the low-energy one.

Baraita de-Melekhet ha-Mishkan, para 26 — ONE witness, uncollated.

In a sandstorm

Desert dust storms are measured at 50–200 kV/m near the surface. Khamsin gusts reach 140 km/h and humidity drops below 5%. The Ark solved as a floating conductor (net charge zero) in a 200 kV/m field:

peak field, at the cherubim261 kV/m
concentration1.31×
of air-breakdown threshold~8.7%

On this geometry it is not doing anything dramatic — short of breakdown by roughly an order of magnitude.

But the cherubim in that mesh are rectangular blocks. Flat faces concentrate field less than curves or points, so 1.31× is a lower bound. Real hammered wings have not been solved. And the 3 MV/m threshold is clean air at sea level — at 1569 m, hot and dust-laden it is lower, which narrows the margin further. Neither is sourced.

The air inside

Half a log of oil per lamp, seven lamps, dusk to dawn — 1.2–2.1 L of olive oil a night, in a closed gold-lined tent:

continuous heat1000–1700 W
CO2 (limit 5,000 ppm)9,600–16,800 ppm
O2 (deficient <19.5%)19.3–18.2%
water added7.8–13.6 g/m³

That last line matters: the menorah is a humidifier, running nightly inside a structure whose electrical behaviour is moisture-controlled.

Unventilated bound, not a prediction — and a loose one. Nothing in the text seals the coverings to the ground.

Menachot 89a (oil) · Keritot 6a (incense) — one witness each.

The incense

368 maneh a year, “365 corresponding to the days of the solar year” — one maneh a day, split morning and evening. That is 0.78–1.56 g/m³ in the Holy Place. The laboratory study that achieved total microbial kill with frankincense smoke used 137.8 g/m³.

The Tabernacle is 88–176× more dilute. Room fumigation is not the mechanism at this scale.

Gold

Above 98% infrared reflectance from ~700 nm, ~99% in the far infrared, and it does not tarnish. Every interior surface is gold; the only one that is not is the floor, which the text never specifies. So the Holy Place is a near-mirror cavity with seven flames in it and bare ground as the only absorber. No radiative exchange has been solved yet.

If it holds charge

Every result above constrained the Ark to net charge zero — induction only. That was a modelling choice, and the environment argues against it. Charging mechanisms here are established and measured: blown sand is triboelectrically charged (that is the documented origin of the 50–200 kV/m dust-storm fields), so a gold shell sandblasted at 140 km/h in sub-5% humidity exchanges charge; add ion capture in a high field and chemi-ionization at the seven flames and the censer.

Driving the shell to potential and scaling to breakdown gives the ceiling — the most it can hold before it lets go:

at ~200 kV (ambient scale)1.7 J
ceiling, 1.0 MV/m breakdown38.5 J
ceiling, 2.0 MV/m154 J
ceiling, 3.0 MV/m347 J

That is not the millijoule range of an ordinary static shock.

What is and is not established

Established: the charging mechanisms, the ambient field, the capacitance, the field concentration, and this energy ceiling.

Not computed: the rate — how fast charge accumulates and what equilibrium potential the environment actually drives the Ark to. The ceiling says what it can hold, not what it did. Reaching the top of that table needs megavolt-scale potential and nothing yet shows the environment gets there.

Still unsourced: breakdown in thin, hot, dust-laden air — all three corrections lower it, which lowers the ceiling. No clinical injury threshold, so this page makes no claim about harm. The cherubim are blocks, not wings; real points raise field-per-volt and lower every figure above. The placements are rabbinic, not Torah, on a single uncollated witness.
