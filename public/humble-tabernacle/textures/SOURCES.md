# Photographed material for the coverings — provenance

Added 2026-09-28 (Cisco: "stop playing Minecraft with this tabernacle"). All four sets are
**CC0 1.0 (public domain)** scans from Poly Haven, downloaded at 1K JPG (colour `diff.jpg`,
OpenGL normal `nor.jpg`, roughness `rough.jpg`). Sizes are the scan's real-world extent as
Poly Haven states it; the viewer tiles each set at that size in cubits (450 mm).

| folder | Poly Haven asset | scan size | authors (per Poly Haven) | stands for | text |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `leather_red_02/` | https://polyhaven.com/a/leather_red_02 | 600 × 600 mm | Rob Tuytel | rams' skins dyed red | Ex 26:14 |
| `brown_leather/` | https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_leather | 400 × 400 mm | Rob Tuytel | taḥash hide grain (colour discarded, tinted violet in code) | Ex 26:14 |
| `hessian_230/` | https://polyhaven.com/a/hessian_230 | 269 × 267 mm | colormass (photography), Rico Cilliers (processing) | goats' hair cloth (tinted dark in code) | Ex 26:7 |
| `terlenka/` | https://polyhaven.com/a/terlenka | 266 × 266 mm | colormass (photography), Rico Cilliers (processing) | fine twisted linen (court hangings) | Ex 27:9 |

Download URLs follow the pattern
`https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/<asset>/<asset>_<map>_1k.jpg`
with `<map>` = `diff` (or `coll1` for leather_red_02, `albedo` for brown_leather), `nor_gl`, `rough`.

What these are NOT: evidence. The text names the fibre or the animal and never the grain; the
choice of scan is art direction and is labelled so in `gptab.html`. The inner figured linen with
its cherubim (Ex 26:1) keeps its drawn pattern; no photograph can supply that design.
