The Tabernacle

Where every number comes from

Cubit0.45 m (elbow to fingertip). All sizes below are in cubits.
Boards48 boards, 10 × 1½ (Ex 26:15-25); thickness four fingers (Josephus, Ant. III 6.3). Silver sockets, gold bars.
Linen curtains40 × 28 (Ex 26:1-6). Hang to a cubit above the ground (Philo, Mos. II 85-86; Josephus Ant. III 6.4).
Goats' hair44 × 30 (Ex 26:7-13): doubled at the front (26:9), half a curtain over the back (26:12), a cubit past the linen each side (26:13), so it reaches the ground.
Rams' skins, redExodus gives no size (26:14). Shown to the ground, after Josephus ("like the veil"). Pegged hem: after Casaretto, no early source.
Taḥash skinsNo size in Exodus (26:14). Shown a cubit short so the red shows: a choice, not a text.
Court100 × 50, hangings 5 high, 60 pillars, bronze sockets, silver hooks (Ex 27:9-18).
FurnitureAndres Casaretto's models, fitted to Exodus sizes: Ark 2½×1½×1½, table 2×1×1½, incense altar 1×1×2, bronze altar 5×5×3, laver and lampstand (no size in the text).
Left out on purposeRashi and later commentators (after 476). No ridge pole: the text has none.
