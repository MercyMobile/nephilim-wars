### Chapter 5: The Soul Economy
This is Nephilim Wars' unique moral/spiritual resource system. Convert to PF2e subsystem:

**Righteousness Points (RP)**
- Starting pool based on ancestry and background
- Maximum pool = Level + WIS modifier (minimum 1)
- Expenditure options:
  - Reroll a failed d20 roll (1 RP)
  - Grant ally temporary HP equal to your level (1 RP)
  - Resist corruption effect (automatic, costs 1 RP)
  - Power divine class features (variable cost)
  - Invoke Divine Intervention at high levels (5+ RP)
- Gaining RP:
  - Righteous acts (GM discretion, typically 1 RP)
  - Self-sacrifice for others (1-2 RP)
  - Resisting temptation when mechanically disadvantageous (1 RP)
  - Completing covenant obligations (1-3 RP)
  - Long rest in sanctified ground (restore to half maximum)

**Corruption Points (CP)**
- No maximum (accumulates indefinitely)
- Effects by threshold:
  - 1-3 CP: -1 penalty to Initiative
  - 4-6 CP: -2 Initiative, -1 to saves vs. divine effects
  - 7-9 CP: -3 Initiative, -2 divine saves, demons can sense you
  - 10+ CP: As above, plus risk of Divine Judgment (GM rolls secretly)
- Gaining CP:
  - Using Forbidden Sorcery (Magi class feature, 1 CP per spell level)
  - Acts of cruelty or betrayal (1-3 CP)
  - Breaking sworn oaths (2-5 CP based on severity)
  - Consorting with demons or Watchers (1-3 CP per interaction)
  - Consuming forbidden substances (1 CP)
- Removing CP:
  - Atonement ritual (requires Priest, removes 1d4 CP, once per week)
  - Prophetic quest completion (removes 1d6 CP)
  - Divine Miracle: Purification (high-level Priest ability)
  - CP cannot drop below 0

**Net Righteousness Score**
- Calculated as: RP (current) - CP (total)
- Positive score: Divine favor, bonuses to interactions with righteous NPCs
- Negative score: Divine disfavor, attracts demonic attention
- At -10 or lower: Character becomes NPC villain (retirement)

### Consistency
- Maintain consistent naming conventions
- Use standardized stat block format throughout
- Ensure all cross-references are accurate
- Keep Bronze Age aesthetic in all equipment and terminology
