## Card Art Style Notes

**Consistent Elements:**
- Aspect ratio: 3:4 for portrait card format
- Style: Fantasy card art / MTG-style illustration
- Quality terms: highly detailed, dramatic lighting, epic fantasy illustration
- Painted finish: oil painting style or classical fantasy art

**Color Palettes by Category:**
- Archangels: White, gold, silver, blue, green (holy luminous)
- High Angels: Sapphire blue, brilliant white, purple (transcendent wisdom)
- Angel of Death: Black, deep purple, silver grey (solemn threshold)
- Demons/Fallen: Red, black, purple, corrupted gold (fallen glory)
- Nephilim/Giants: Bronze, umber, sienna, earth tones (ancient power)
- Corrupted Humans: Blood red, shadow black, false gold (twisted worship)

**Background Guidelines:**
- Angels: Heavenly courts, celestial realms, divine light
- Fallen: Deserts, pits, wilderness, chains, void
- Giants: Ancient battlefields, ruins, mountain fortresses
- Corrupted: Pagan altars, throne rooms, shadow
