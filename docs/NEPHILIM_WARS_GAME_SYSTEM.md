# Nephilim Wars: Complete Game System Document

> **Purpose**: This document extracts all game mechanics, rules, classes, races, and lore from the Nephilim Wars digital implementation. It is intended for adaptation to other RPG systems (particularly Pathfinder 2e).

---

## PART I: SETTING & LORE

### The Antediluvian World

The setting takes place in the **pre-Flood era** (before Noah's Deluge), a time when:
- Humanity lived for centuries (lifespans of 700-900+ years)
- Fallen angels called **Watchers** walked the earth
- Their offspring, the **Nephilim** (giants), dominated civilization
- Bronze Age technology mixed with forbidden supernatural knowledge
- Divine judgment looms over a corrupted world

### The Watchers (Fallen Angels)

Two hundred angels descended on **Mount Hermon**, bound by oath under **Semyaza's** leadership. They took human wives and taught forbidden knowledge:

| Watcher | Forbidden Teaching |
|---------|-------------------|
| **Azazel** | Warfare (swords, shields, armor), cosmetics, vanity |
| **Semyaza** | Enchantments and root-cutting (herbalism/poisons) |
| **Baraqel** | Astrology and observation of stars |
| **Kokabiel** | The constellations and their meanings |
| **Shamsiel** | Signs of the sun |
| **Sariel** | Course of the moon |
| **Penemue** | Writing and ink |
| **Kasdeja** | Abortion and striking the embryo |

**Divine Punishment**: The Watchers were bound and imprisoned beneath the earth until the Day of Judgment. Azazel specifically was bound in Dudael beneath jagged rocks.

### The Nephilim (Giants)

The offspring of Watchers and human women. Key characteristics:
- Called "Mighty Men of Old, Men of Renown" (Genesis 6:4)
- First generation: 12-15+ feet tall
- Consumed enormous resources, eventually turning to cannibalism
- When killed, their immortal angelic souls cannot die—they become **wandering demon spirits** (Refa'im)

**Giant Lineages/Generations**:
1. **Nephilim** (1st Gen) - Direct Watcher offspring, largest (12-15 ft)
2. **Rephaim** - Later generation, associated with death/shades (9-11 ft)
3. **Anakim** - "Long-Necked Ones," noble bearing, chain-wearers (10-13 ft)
4. **Gibborim** - "Mighty Men," human-giant hybrids (6.5-8 ft)
5. **Horim** - Cave-dwelling giant-kin (6-7 ft)

### Human Lineages

| Lineage | Origin | Characteristics |
|---------|--------|-----------------|
| **Sethite** | Descendants of Seth (Adam's son) | Keepers of original faith, righteous line leading to Noah |
| **Cainite** | Descendants of Cain | City builders, metallurgists, musicians; bear Cain's mark |
| **Wanderer** | Rejected both paths | Nomadic peoples, survivalists |
| **Sorcerer Clan** | Watcher-taught humans | Initiated into forbidden mysteries |

- **Book of Giants** (Dead Sea Scrolls - 4Q203, 4Q530-532)

## PART III: CORE MECHANICS

### Attributes

The six core attributes (standard d20 system):

| Attribute | Abbreviation | Governs |
|-----------|--------------|---------|
| Strength | STR | Melee attacks, carrying capacity, athletics |
| Dexterity | DEX | Ranged attacks, AC, initiative, stealth |
| Constitution | CON | Hit points, endurance, fortitude |
| Intelligence | INT | Knowledge, arcane magic, reasoning |
| Wisdom | WIS | Perception, divine magic, willpower |
| Charisma | CHA | Social interaction, leadership, presence |

**Modifier Calculation**: (Score - 10) ÷ 2, rounded down

| Score | Modifier |
|-------|----------|
| 1 | -5 |
| 2-3 | -4 |
| 4-5 | -3 |
| 6-7 | -2 |
| 8-9 | -1 |
| 10-11 | +0 |
| 12-13 | +1 |
| 14-15 | +2 |
| 16-17 | +3 |
| 18-19 | +4 |
| 20+ | +5+ |

### Combat System

#### Basic Attack Resolution
```
d20 + Attack Bonus ≥ Target Defense = HIT
```

- **Attack Bonus** = Proficiency Bonus + Attribute Modifier (STR for melee, DEX for ranged)
- **Defense (AC)** = 10 + DEX Modifier + Armor Bonus + Shield Bonus
- **Proficiency Bonus** = +2 (increases with level in standard d20 progression)

#### Critical Hits & Fumbles
- **Natural 20**: Automatic hit, double all damage dice
- **Natural 1**: Automatic miss (optional: fumble consequence)

#### Damage Resolution
```
[Weapon Damage Dice] + [Attribute Modifier] + [Other Bonuses]
```

#### Damage Types
| Type | Description | Common Sources |
|------|-------------|----------------|
| Slashing | Cutting damage | Swords, axes, claws |
| Piercing | Puncture damage | Arrows, spears, daggers |
| Bludgeoning | Impact damage | Hammers, clubs, fists |
| Fire | Heat/flame | Watcher magic, divine judgment |
| Cold | Freezing | Ice magic |
| Lightning | Electrical | Storm magic |
| Radiant | Holy light | Divine miracles, angelic |
| Necrotic | Death energy | Demonic, corruption |
| Poison | Toxic | Serpents, root-cutting |
| Psychic | Mental | Mind magic |
| Thunder | Sonic | Divine word |
| Force | Pure energy | Arcane magic |

#### Initiative
```
d20 + DEX Modifier - (Corruption Points penalty, if applicable)
```

Higher initiative acts first. Ties broken by higher DEX score.

#### Hit Points
```
Base HP = 10 + CON Modifier + Racial HP Bonus
Per Level = Class Hit Die + CON Modifier
```

### The Soul Economy (Unique Mechanic)

This is the game's distinctive moral/spiritual tracking system.

#### Righteousness Points (RP)
- **Represents**: Virtue, alignment with divine will, moral integrity
- **Starting Amount**: Determined by race (Sethites start with +2)
- **Gaining RP**:
  - Acts of genuine righteousness
  - Self-sacrifice for others
  - Resisting corruption
  - Keeping covenants/oaths
- **Spending RP**:
  - Reroll any failed d20 roll
  - Heal an ally
  - Resist corruption effects
  - Power divine miracles

#### Corruption Points (CP)
- **Represents**: Compromise with forbidden powers, moral degradation
- **Starting Amount**: Determined by race (Nephilim start with +3)
- **Gaining CP**:
  - Using forbidden Watcher magic
  - Acts of violence/cruelty
  - Breaking sacred oaths
  - Consorting with fallen beings
- **Mechanical Effects**:
  - High CP reduces Initiative (sluggish spirit)
  - May trigger divine judgment effects
  - Opens vulnerability to demonic influence
  - Social consequences with righteous NPCs

#### Balance Mechanic
- **Net Righteousness** = RP - CP
- Positive = Favored by the Divine
- Negative = Marked for judgment
- Zero = Neutral, on the edge

## PART VI: MAGIC SYSTEMS

### Forbidden Sorcery (Magi)

Magic taught by the Watchers. Using this magic risks gaining Corruption Points.

**Sample Spells**:
- **Azazel's Flame**: 3d6 fire damage, 20 ft radius (CP risk: 1)
- **Semyaza's Charm**: Target must save or be charmed for 1 hour (CP risk: 1)
- **Baraqel's Star Reading**: Gain advantage on next 3 rolls (CP risk: 0)
- **Root Cutting**: Create poisons or healing draughts (CP risk varies)
- **Word of Binding**: Paralyze target for 1 minute (CP risk: 2)

### Divine Miracles (Priest)

Power granted through faith in the Creator. Using miracles may require RP expenditure.

**Sample Miracles**:
- **Healing Touch**: Restore 2d8+WIS HP (RP cost: 0-1)
- **Shield of Faith**: +2 AC to ally for 10 minutes (RP cost: 0)
- **Turn Unholy**: Spirits and demons must flee (RP cost: 1)
- **Divine Judgment**: 4d6 radiant damage to corrupted target (RP cost: 2)
- **Sanctuary**: Create protected area demons cannot enter (RP cost: 1)

## PART VII: CAMPAIGN THEMES

### Central Conflicts

1. **Righteousness vs. Corruption**: The moral struggle as the world grows darker
2. **Giants vs. Humanity**: Nephilim oppression and human resistance
3. **Forbidden Knowledge**: The temptation and danger of Watcher teachings
4. **Coming Judgment**: The looming Flood and divine reckoning
5. **Survival**: Living in a world dominated by supernatural evil

### Adventure Hooks

- Hunting demon-spirits (Refa'im) that possess villagers
- Seeking Enoch's wisdom before his translation to heaven
- Raiding a Watcher's abandoned teaching site for forbidden knowledge
- Protecting Noah's family as they build the Ark
- Infiltrating a Cainite city to rescue prisoners meant for sacrifice
- Tracking a Nephilim warlord across the antediluvian wilderness
- Deciphering prophecies about the coming Flood
- Breaking the chains that bind a lesser Watcher to gain information

### Tone Guidelines

- **Apocalyptic**: The world is ending; everyone knows judgment is coming
- **Bronze Age Grit**: Primitive technology, harsh survival
- **Mythic Scale**: Heroes and monsters of legend

## APPENDIX: CONVERSION NOTES FOR PATHFINDER 2E

### Why Pathfinder 2e May Fit Better

1. **Three-Action Economy**: Better represents the tactical combat between humans and giants
2. **Ancestry Feats**: Perfect for representing different giant lineages and human cultures
3. **Proficiency System**: TEML (Trained/Expert/Master/Legendary) better models the spectrum from mortal to divine
4. **Conditions & Afflictions**: More granular corruption/possession mechanics possible
5. **Encounter Building**: CR system better handles mixed-tier parties (humans + giants)
6. **Class Feats**: Allow better customization of Watcher-taught abilities

**Ancestries**:
- Sethite, Cainite, Wanderer → Human heritages with unique ancestry feats

- Magi → Sorcerer (Watcher Bloodline - new) or Wizard
- Priest → Cleric or Champion
- Artisan → Inventor (if using Guns & Gears) or Alchemist
