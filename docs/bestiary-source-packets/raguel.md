# Raguel — Ancient Witness Source Packet

## Editorial judgment

Raguel's secure dossier is confined to two connected Enochic notices. He belongs to the seven holy watchers, with a difficult office concerning the world of the luminaries, and he personally explains to Enoch the unceasing western fire associated with those luminaries. The texts do not place him in a celestial courtroom or give him scales, a measuring rod, armor, blue-and-gold robes, or authority over all fellow angels.

## Name

- Raguel/Ragouel is conventionally related to Hebrew רְעוּאֵל, "friend of God," but the familiar biblical Reuel is a human name and is not evidence for this angel's deeds.

## 1 Enoch 20:4

In the list of seven holy angels who watch, Charles prints Raguel as the angel who "†takes vengeance on† the world of the luminaries." The daggers flag textual uncertainty. Laurence renders him as the angel "who inflicts punishment on the world and the luminaries." The underlying office concerns correction or punishment within the luminary realm, but its exact English formulation is not secure.

## 1 Enoch 23

At the western ends of the earth, Enoch sees a burning fire that runs without resting, regular by day and night. Raguel, one of the accompanying holy angels, explains: the course of fire is the western fire that "†persecutes† all the luminaries of heaven" in Charles; Laurence calls it the blazing fire running toward the west, "that of all the luminaries of heaven." Again the marked verb is uncertain, while Raguel's eyewitness role as interpreter of the unceasing luminary fire is secure.

Primary witnesses: `docs/scribe-texts/bib/boe/boe023.md`, `boe026.md`; variant translation `docs/scribe-texts/bib/bep/bep02.md`, `bep03.md`.

## Claims bounded

- "Angel of justice" is a reasonable summary of corrective office, not a quoted ancient title.
- "Enforcer among angels" overstates the texts; the objects named are luminaries.
- No scales, court, throne, measuring rod, armor, weapon, wings, halo, or individualized anatomy is supplied.
- Solomonic and magical correspondences are unnecessary and excluded. Crazy Alchemist was used only for discovery; the storybook supplies no controlling Raguel witness.

## Artwork verdict

Replace the celestial-court fantasy with 1 Enoch 23: an unwinged, humanlike Raguel beside Enoch at the western end of the earth, indicating the vast regular river/course of fire that never rests and is associated with the heavenly luminaries. Humanlike form is a conservative visual inference, not attested anatomy.

Required restraints: no scales, rod, throne, armor, sword, wings, halo, seals, zodiac, or modern astronomical notation; the westward fire and ordered luminaries must dominate; shared encaustic/egg-tempera Ancient Witness style.
