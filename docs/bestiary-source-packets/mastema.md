# Mastema — The Tenth of Spirits Source Packet

Jubilees 10 presents Mastema as chief of the malignant spirits. After Noah prays for relief and God orders the spirits bound, Mastema petitions to retain some for corruption and judgment because human wickedness is great. God leaves one tenth under him and sends nine tenths to condemnation; an angel teaches Noah herbal remedies against their diseases and seductions. Later Jubilees has Mastema propose Abraham's test, stand shamed at Isaac's deliverance, oppose Moses, aid Egypt's sorcerers, and suffer temporary restraint. His action is permitted and bounded.

Primary local witnesses: `docs/scribe-texts/bib/jub/jub24.md`, `jub39.md`, `jub40.md`, and `jub85.md`.

Mastema means hostility/enmity and may designate the prince or hostile spirits collectively. Jubilees gives no horns, red body, throne, crown, seal, weapon, or equal rivalry with God.

Artwork: before overwhelming veiled divine light, an unresolved humanlike dark prince kneels in petition while a host of shadow spirits divides—nine great streams descending to sealed confinement and one visibly smaller tenth remaining behind him. At the side, a plain radiant angel gives Noah gathered medicinal herbs. God is not bodily depicted; light and division express authority.

Exclude bodily God, horned devil, crown, trident, hell throne, equal anti-god, triumphant pose, magic seal, chained humans, medieval court, or readable text. Shared Ancient Witness encaustic/egg-tempera style.
