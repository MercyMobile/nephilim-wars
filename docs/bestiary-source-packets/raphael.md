# Raphael — Ancient Witness Source Packet

## Editorial judgment

Raphael is not merely a symbolic "celestial physician." Tobit gives the fullest eyewitness-style narrative: he passes as the hired kinsman Azariah, walks and eats with a family, instructs Tobias in practical remedies, binds Asmodeus, handles money, negotiates a marriage, returns the traveler safely, and only afterward reveals that he is one of the seven who stand before God. 1 Enoch independently joins healing to violent restraint: Raphael binds Azazel under jagged stones so that the corrupted earth may be healed.

## Name

- Hebrew: רְפָאֵל (*Refa'el*), "God heals" / "God has healed," from רפא and אל.
- Tobit itself interprets the mission rather than the etymology: Raphael says God sent him to heal Tobit and deliver Sarah.

## Tobit 3–12

- God hears the simultaneous prayers of the blinded Tobit and demon-tormented Sarah and sends Raphael to heal both (3:16–17 in many recensions; verse numbering varies).
- Raphael appears as a young man and gives the human identity "Azariah son of the great Hananiah." Tobit hires him as guide (5).
- At the Tigris, a fish attacks Tobias. Raphael orders him to seize it and preserve heart, liver, and gall; the organs become the means of deliverance and healing (6).
- Smoke from heart and liver drives off Asmodeus; Raphael pursues and binds the demon in Upper Egypt (8:2–3).
- Raphael retrieves Gabael's deposited money and returns with the party (9–11).
- Fish gall removes the white films from Tobit's eyes under Raphael's instruction (11).
- Raphael reveals that he presented their prayers, was sent to heal and deliver, and is one of the seven angels who stand ready before God's glory (12:12–15). The family falls face-down in fear.
- Raphael says his apparent eating and drinking were part of the manifestation; textual forms differ on the exact wording. The disguise is human-passing, with no wings or medical insignia.

## 1 Enoch

- **9:** Raphael joins Michael, Uriel, and Gabriel in seeing human bloodshed and bringing the dead's cry before the Most High.
- **10:4–8:** God orders Raphael to bind Azazel hand and foot, cast him into a desert opening at Dudael, cover him with rough stones and darkness, and heal the earth corrupted by the Watchers' disclosures.
- **20:3:** Raphael is one of the holy angels who watch and is set over the spirits of men.
- **22:** Raphael guides Enoch through the hollow places where human spirits await judgment and identifies Abel's accusing spirit.
- **32:** Raphael interprets the fragrant tree of wisdom to Enoch.
- **40:9:** he is set over the diseases and wounds of humankind.
- **54:6; 68; 71:** he joins the four presences in judgment scenes and receives Michael's grief-stricken words about judgment's severity.

## Jubilees 10 boundary

Jubilees says God commands "one of us" to teach Noah medicines against the surviving malignant spirits, but the body text does not name Raphael. R. H. Charles's footnote identifies that angel as Raphael by comparison with the Book of Noah and Tobit. Therefore Jubilees may be cited as a parallel healing tradition, not as an explicit Raphael occurrence. Current-card wording that names "Jubilees 10:10" as direct Raphael evidence must be qualified.

## Later and magical layers

Later Jewish and Christian reception expands Raphael's associations. Solomonic and magical tables variously assign air, east, Mercury, Sun, particular colors, seals, and incenses. These systems conflict and are unnecessary because Tobit supplies a concrete visible form and tools. None is imported into the core portrait.

## Artwork verdict

Use Tobit's Tigris encounter, not a generic winged healer: Raphael as the unwinged, human-passing traveling companion "Azariah," calmly directing Tobias beside the seized fish, with the dog and travel gear present. The organs may be prepared or held in a small cloth, but Raphael should not wear modern medical symbols. This scene shows guidance, practical healing knowledge, disguise, and the beginning of the family's deliverance.

Required restraints:

- no wings, halo, caduceus, physician's coat, glowing staff, potion bottles, armor, or Solomonic seal;
- plausible ancient Median/Assyrian travel clothing and equipment;
- Raphael must pass fully as a young human traveler;
- fish, Tigris bank, Tobias, dog, and journey gear anchor the Tobit witness;
- shared Ancient Witness Bestiary encaustic/egg-tempera narrative realism.
