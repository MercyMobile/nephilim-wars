# Armaros — Resolving Enchantments Source Packet

## Primary witness

- **1 Enoch 6:7:** Armaros (variant forms include Armaros/Armaros and Armers across Ethiopic and Greek transmission) is named among the chiefs of the two hundred Watchers who swear the shared oath on Hermon.
- **1 Enoch 8:3:** the teaching catalogue assigns him “the resolving of enchantments” or “solution of sorcery.” The concise phrase is preserved locally in `docs/scribe-texts/bib/boe/boe011.md` and the variant rendering in `bib/bep/bep02.md`.
- The text distinguishes this from Semyaza's enchantments/root-cuttings. Armaros teaches how such workings are undone; it does not say he invented all magic.

## Textual boundary

No early witness supplies an individual adventure, bodily description, weapon, seal, element, planetary office, or later demonic rank. Even “counter-magic” is a modern shorthand for the specific Enochic phrase and should not be expanded into benevolent healing: he remains among the condemned teachers whose disclosures corrupt the earth.

## Artwork verdict

Show forbidden instruction at human scale. At night outside an early highland settlement, an imposing humanlike Watcher in a plain ancient mantle demonstrates to awed human pupils how a complex knotted cord-and-herb binding is loosened and dismantled on a flat stone. His hands and the unraveling object are central; a faint unnatural radiance marks his otherness, but exact anatomy remains unresolved. Hermon's dark ridge and the distant silhouettes of other descended Watchers connect him to the oath.

Required exclusions: wizard staff, spell blast, magic circle, pentagram, sigils, readable text, grimoire, demon horns, red skin, fantasy armor, weapon, benevolent healer halo, or modern occult costume. Shared Ancient Witness encaustic/egg-tempera style.
