# Saraqiel — Ancient Witness Source Packet

## Editorial judgment

The existing "Sariel / Suriel" archangel card conflates at least two figures in 1 Enoch. Chapter 8 places **Sariêl** among the rebellious teachers and says he taught the course of the moon. Chapter 20 lists the holy watcher **Saraqâêl**, set over spirits who sin in the spirit. Similar names do not authorize collapsing a fallen teacher into a holy overseer. The card should therefore be normalized to Saraqiel and limited to the chapter 20 office. A separate Sariêl card may later be added among the fallen Watchers.

## Secure early witness

### 1 Enoch 20:1, 6

The text introduces "the names of the holy angels who watch" and identifies Saraqâêl as one "set over the spirits, who sin in the spirit." This supplies:

- membership among the seven named holy watchers;
- a delegated supervisory or custodial office;
- responsibility concerning spirits whose offense is itself spiritual.

It supplies no narrative action, physical description, weapon, element, direction, planet, lunar authority, or title "Angel of Death."

Primary witness: R. H. Charles, `docs/scribe-texts/bib/boe/boe023.md`.

## The Sariêl distinction

### 1 Enoch 8:3

In Charles's translation, Sariêl appears within the catalogue of illicit Watcher teaching and teaches "the course of the moon." The passage follows the descent and corruption narrative and culminates in humanity's destruction and cry to heaven. Laurence's older translation instead reads **Asaradel** for the lunar teacher, demonstrating genuine name instability.

Sources: `docs/scribe-texts/bib/boe/boe011.md`; variant `docs/scribe-texts/bib/bep/bep02.md`.

The lunar-teacher detail cannot be moved onto holy Saraqiel without argument. This dossier keeps the figures separate. The current game-system assertion that Sariel simply owns "Course of the moon" should be revisited when the fallen Watcher roster is audited.

## Suriel and other variants

Suriel, Sariel, Suryal, Saraqael, and Saraqiel can overlap in later lists and translations, sometimes also colliding with Uriel. The local Laurence translation even adds both Suryal and Uriel to the appeal scene of 1 Enoch 9 where Charles gives Michael, Uriel, Raphael, and Gabriel. These shifting lists are textual evidence, not permission to build a composite biography.

## Claims rejected from the old card

- **Angel of Death:** not stated in either cited Enoch passage.
- **Teacher of lunar courses:** belongs to Sariêl/Asaradel in the fallen-teacher list, not securely to holy Saraqiel.
- **Taught Moses the lunar calendar:** absent from the cited local primary witnesses and must not be presented as Enochic testimony.
- **Avenger of spirits:** chapter 20 says "set over" spirits who sin in spirit; it does not describe vengeance or its mechanism.

## Later magical boundary

No Solomonic correspondence is needed to fill the record. Late seals, colors, planets, weapons, and directions would create a portrait more confident than the ancient text permits. Crazy Alchemist was used only as a discovery aid. The storybook was checked and supplies no controlling Saraqiel witness.

## Artwork verdict

The existing moonlit, winged figure with scales is rejected because every major feature comes from the conflation or later iconography. The most honest replacement is a deliberately restrained scene of the seven holy watchers as humanlike presences, with Saraqiel foregrounded at the boundary of a dark holding-place of disordered spirits. This is a visual inference from the office, not a claimed eyewitness anatomy, and the card must say so.

Required restraints:

- no moon symbolism, scales, wings, halo, sword, armor, crown, or magical seal;
- no depiction of Saraqiel as the fallen lunar teacher;
- Saraqiel appears humanlike only as a conservative messenger convention, explicitly marked inferential;
- the disordered spirits remain indistinct and non-sensational;
- shared Ancient Witness Bestiary encaustic/egg-tempera narrative realism.
