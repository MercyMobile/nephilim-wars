# Gadreel — Eve's Deceiver and Weapons of Death Source Packet

## Primary witness: 1 Enoch 69:6–7

- The later Enochic list names Gadreel as the one who led Eve astray.
- The same passage says he showed humanity “all the blows/weapons of death,” then specifies shield, coat of mail/breastplate, sword for battle, and the full equipment of death.
- The narrator concludes that these weapons brought bloodshed upon the earth.

Primary translation checked locally at `docs/scribe-texts/bib/boe/boe072.md` with variant witness at `bib/bep/bep05.md`.

## Layer boundary

Genesis 3 calls the deceiver “the serpent” and never names Gadreel. 1 Enoch 69 supplies a later identification; the card must state that rather than harmonizing Gadreel, Satan, and the Genesis serpent into one biography. The passage does not say Gadreel has a snake body, entered a snake, or forged the weapons himself. It gives no wings, armor of his own, battle, seal, rank, or Solomonic office.

## Artwork verdict

Use a divided narrative field. The larger field shows Gadreel as a plain humanlike Watcher revealing a newly made shield, scale coat, and sword to ancient men while blood-red sunset reflects in a distant river—consequence without gore. A smaller, hazy garden-memory field shows Eve at a fruiting tree listening uneasily to a humanlike shadow partly hidden beyond the trunk; no literal serpent anatomy is claimed. The division visibly marks Enoch's later retrospective identification.

Required exclusions: Gadreel as snake-man, Satan label, apple, nude Eve, sexualized garden, winged armored warrior portrait, battle action, corpses, gore, magic seal, readable text, medieval plate armor. Shared Ancient Witness encaustic/egg-tempera style.
