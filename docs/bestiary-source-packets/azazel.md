# Azazel — Leviticus, Enoch, and Abraham Source Packet

## Editorial judgment

Azazel must be presented as a layered figure. Leviticus 16 sends the living goat “for Azazel” into the wilderness but does not describe Azazel's body, biography, or identity as a Watcher. The Book of Watchers names Azazel/Asael among the descended angels, makes him the chief revealer of weapons, metalwork, ornament, cosmetics, stones, and dyes, and gives a precise punishment scene in Dudael. The later *Apocalypse of Abraham* develops Azazel as a wilderness adversary. “Scapegoat” is the goat or ritual function in English tradition, not a secure translation of the proper name.

## Leviticus 16

- Two goats stand before YHWH at the tent entrance. Lots designate one “for YHWH” and one “for Azazel” (16:7–10).
- Aaron lays both hands on the living goat, confesses Israel's iniquities over it, and sends it by an appointed man into the wilderness; the goat bears the iniquities to a cut-off land (16:20–22).
- The text does not say the goat *is* Azazel, depict a demon receiving it, or explain the term's etymology. Ancient and modern interpretations include a wilderness being, a place/destination, and removal; the ambiguity must remain visible.

## 1 Enoch 6–16

- 8:1: Azazel teaches humans swords, knives, shields, breastplates, metals and their working, bracelets, ornaments, antimony and beautifying the eyelids, costly stones, and dyes. The offense is broader than “patron of warfare.”
- 9:6 and 10:8 place extraordinary blame on Azazel for revealing heavenly secrets and corrupting the earth.
- 10:4–6: Raphael is commanded to bind him hand and foot, cast him into darkness in the desert Dudael, heap rough and jagged rocks over him, and cover his face so he cannot see light; at great judgment he will be cast into fire.
- 13:1–3: Enoch personally announces that Azazel will have no peace because of the unrighteousness he taught. The Watchers collectively seek a petition afterward.

## Textual boundaries

- Leviticus does not state that its Azazel is the Enochic Watcher, though the Book of Watchers' desert punishment likely participates in an interpretive relationship with the ritual.
- The name varies as Azazel and Asael across witnesses. Its etymology is disputed; “Scapegoat” is not a safe personal-name translation.
- The *Apocalypse of Abraham* is a later witness and may support an expanded adversarial dossier, but it does not control the older Leviticus ritual or the Book of Watchers punishment scene.
- Modern summaries that reduce the teaching to metallurgy omit cosmetics, jewelry, stones, and dyes. These instruments of violence and alluring display belong together in the text.

## Artwork verdict

Replace the warrior portrait with 1 Enoch 10's eyewitness-grade punishment: in the stony desert ravine of Dudael, Raphael binds a humanlike Azazel hand and foot while jagged rocks close over him; a rough covering obscures Azazel's face from the light. Broken swords, a shield, metal ornaments, antimony vessel, colored mineral pigments, and worked stones lie abandoned at the ravine edge as restrained evidence of his teachings.

Required exclusions: goat head, making Azazel the Levitical goat, horned demon anatomy, triumphant armor, flaming sword duel, medieval hell, or treating the wilderness goat ritual as a sacrifice to a visible idol. Shared Ancient Witness encaustic/egg-tempera style.
