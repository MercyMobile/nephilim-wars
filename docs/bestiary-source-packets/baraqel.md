# Baraqel — Astrological Instruction Source Packet

## Primary witness

- **1 Enoch 6:7:** Baraqel/Baraqijal appears among the chiefs of the Watchers bound by the Hermon oath. Name spellings vary across the Ethiopic and Greek witnesses.
- **1 Enoch 8:3:** the teaching list assigns him the knowledge of astrologers/astrology, adjacent to Kokabiel's constellations and other Watchers' readings of cloud, earth, sun, and moon.
- The Hebrew/Aramaic name element *baraq* means lightning, but the surviving narrative does not say that Baraqel hurls, rules, or embodies lightning.

## Textual boundary

The Book of Giants identifies Mahaway as the son of Baraqel in commonly accepted reconstructions, but that distinct family notice does not add bodily form or weather power to the father. No early text gives Baraqel a weapon, zodiac throne, planetary dominion, armor, color, seal, or individual battle.

## Artwork verdict

Show the prohibited lesson: on a clear highland night below Hermon, a tall humanlike Watcher guides ancient pupils in correlating the slow paths of stars and planets with a set of plain movable stones laid on dark cloth. He points to the real sky while one pupil repositions stones; the method is observation and disclosure, not glowing zodiac magic. A remote lightning flicker behind Hermon may echo the name only as atmosphere, never as a controlled power.

Required exclusions: zodiac wheel, horoscope glyphs, readable chart, lightning weapon or command pose, planet or star held in hand, wizard staff, magic circle, wings, horns, fantasy armor, observatory telescope, modern instruments. Shared Ancient Witness encaustic/egg-tempera style.
