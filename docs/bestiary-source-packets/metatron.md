# Metatron — Hekhalot and Rabbinic Witness Source Packet

## Editorial judgment

Metatron is not named in Genesis and cannot be placed there as an implied biblical angel. His detailed dossier belongs to late-antique/early-medieval Hekhalot literature, especially 3 Enoch, and to rabbinic passages that police the danger of treating his delegated rank as divinity. Unlike the sparse early archangel cards, this tradition supplies extravagant anatomy and should be depicted on its own textual terms.

## Name boundary

- Hebrew/Aramaic: מטטרון. The etymology is disputed; "Guardian" is not secure and should not be stated as a translation.
- Genesis 5:24 says God took Enoch; it neither names Metatron nor describes angelic transformation.

## 3 Enoch / Sefer Hekhalot

- Metatron meets Rabbi Ishmael as servant and Prince of the Divine Presence and protects him among hostile throne beings (1–3).
- He identifies himself as Enoch son of Jared, taken from the flood generation and elevated to serve the throne/chariot and needs of the Shekinah (4–7).
- His flesh becomes flame, sinews blazing fire, bones juniper coals, eyelashes lightning, eyeballs fiery torches, hair hot flames, limbs wings of burning fire, and bodily substance blazing fire (15 in common numbering; transformation sequence spans 3–15).
- God gives him seventy-two wings—thirty-six on each side—each covering the world, and 365,000 eyes, each like the Great Light (9).
- He receives a throne at the door of the seventh palace and authority to judge heavenly households; princes call him "Youth." Some textual strata call him "lesser YHWH," explicitly because God's name is in him, a statement of delegated naming within this literature rather than identity with God.
- Metatron is associated with heavenly records and the deeds of Israel, but "Voice of God" is not a safe universal title.

## Babylonian Talmud, Hagigah 15a

Elisha ben Abuyah sees Metatron permitted to sit and write Israel's merits and concludes there may be "two powers." The response is corrective: Metatron receives sixty fiery lashes and his authority to erase Elisha's merits is described. The story insists that extraordinary delegated office does not make Metatron a second god.

Primary Talmud text checked locally in the Hagigah corpus and against the bilingual witness at `https://www.sefaria.org/Chagigah.15a.6`.

## Boundaries

- Biblical Enoch and Hekhalot Metatron must be distinguished by textual layer.
- "Lesser YHWH" is reported with its explicit delegated-name context and rabbinic anti-dualism; it is not paraphrased as divinity.
- The enormous wing/eye counts are text-specific visionary magnitude, not ordinary bird anatomy.
- Later "Metatron's Cube," occult seals, planetary correspondences, and New Age geometry are absent from these witnesses and excluded.
- Crazy Alchemist was discovery only; the storybook supplies no controlling witness.

## Artwork verdict

Replace the generic robed scribe with the 3 Enoch transformation witnessed by Metatron's own speech: a colossal humanoid body constituted of flame and coals, seventy-two wings suggested as ranks of world-filling burning pinions, and fields of sun-bright eyes distributed through the fiery form, before the seventh palace. The impossible counts should be conveyed through receding multiplicity rather than literally sacrificing readability. A small Rabbi Ishmael establishes visionary scale.

Required restraints: no Metatron cube, occult seal, modern sacred geometry, armor, sword, feathered white church wings, or implication of deity; fiery anatomy and many eyes/wings must be unmistakable; shared encaustic/egg-tempera Ancient Witness style, intensified for the Hekhalot layer.
