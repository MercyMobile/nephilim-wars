# Technical Specification: The City of Enoch (The Titan-Scale Bastion)

**Date:** 2026-04-11
**Topic:** Fleshing out the City of Enoch based on Cainite and Watcher Lore.
**Status:** Approved

## 1. Architectural Vision
The City of Enoch is a hybrid of Bronze Age Sumerian (Eridu/Uruk) architecture and "Titan-Scale" monumentalism. It is designed to serve as both a fortress for the Cainite line and a cosmic laboratory for the Watchers.

### 1.1 Scaling & Materials
- **Nephilim Standard:** 1st-Gen Nephilim (15ft) require 30ft vertical clearance and 10ft wide passageways.
- **Human Inset:** Humans (Sethites/Cainites) use secondary smaller apertures (7ft) and elevated walkways (10ft above ground level) to avoid being crushed by Nephilim traffic.
- **Materials:** Sandstone blocks (base), River-Onyx (paving/industrial), Gold-Leaf (Ziggurat/Elite areas), and Obsidian (Watcher labs).

## 2. City Districts (The Societal Pillars)

### 2.1 The Ziggurat of Semjaza (Celestial Hub)
- **Top Tier:** The "Watcher Platform." Includes a gold-inlaid astronomical floor (The Watcher Grid) for tracking the 36 Decans and cosmic alignments.
- **Lower Tiers:** High-priest chambers and botanical gardens for "root-cutting" (enchantments and pharmacology).
- **Function:** Hub of city magic and astrological divination.

### 2.2 The Tubal-Cain Industrial Sector (The Iron Gut)
- **The Song of the Sword Forges:** Massive open-air forges near the river. Features "forbidden heat" techniques taught by Azazel for bronze and iron smithing.
- **Logistics:** Jabal’s nomadic livestock pens surround this district to provide the high-caloric intake required for the labor force.
- **Weaponry:** Dedicated forges for swords, breastplates, and "Song of the Sword" enchanted blades.

### 2.3 Jubal’s Sonic Conservatory (The Pulse)
- **Infrastructure:** A district of resonant chambers and amphitheaters.
- **Lore Integration:** Uses "Sonic Seduction" (pipes and lyres) to maintain population control and lure Sethites from the mountains.
- **Aesthetic:** Characterized by vibrating onyx pillars and constant rhythmic percussion.

### 2.4 Lamech’s Vengeance Barracks (Military Quarter)
- **Fortification:** Houses built as isolated, defensible towers with high-level slit windows (Cain’s fear of retribution).
- **Doctrine:** The "Seventy-Sevenfold" training grounds for Nephilim-heavy infantry.
- **Gear:** Storage for the Azazel-style shields and armor described in the lore extraction.

## 3. Defensive Systems (Watcher Integration)
- **Megalithic Walls:** Enchanted by Armaros to "resolve" (break) incoming magical or divine intercessions.
- **The Mark Gates:** Entrances featuring Baraqiel’s astrological omens. Passing through these gates acts as a registration of "Weight and Measure" (Cain’s census).
